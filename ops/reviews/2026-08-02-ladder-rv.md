## The outcome

Promotion needs **all four**.

- **`live`** — four PASS.
- **Discard** — gate 1's interval lies entirely below zero.
- **Extend** — only if the sign is right and only precision is missing, **and** a named new
  source of evidence exists. **The July board universe is exhausted**, so an extension means
  the August cohort: a *different regime*, and therefore a **new trial with a new
  pre-registration**, which must be called that and not a continuation.
- **`parked`** — mechanism validated, untradeable. What `arena-rank/favourite-shrinkage` got.
  Available for gate 4 failures. **Not available as a way to avoid recording a negative
  outcome.**

## Arguments disqualified in advance

Both were written down on 2026-07-30 because both will be tempting on the day:

1. **The tail is not excludable.** Same barrier structure, same pricer, same legs we would have

   rows we could actually trade and keeping the ones nobody would fill inverts the whole point
   of `wiki/reference/midpoint-is-not-a-fill.md`.
2. **"We would have no live strategies left" is not an argument.** The firm has one trial and
   zero live variants. That is exactly why the rule was written in advance.
