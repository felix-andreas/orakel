## Gate 5 — the incumbent (ran first, as instructed). **FAILED. This is the kill.**

The idea's author could not obtain an external line and flagged it as the best kill shot.
**We obtained one.** Three independent routes, none requiring an account:

| source | what it is | route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pinnacle** | the sharp esports book | `guest.api.arcadia.pinnacle.com/0.1/sports/12/matchups` + `/matchups/{id}/markets/related/straight`, public guest key, HTTP 200 |
| **Smarkets** | a betting **exchange** — back/lay mid has *no vig at all* | `api.smarkets.com/v3/events/?type=esports&state=upcoming` → `/markets/` → `/quotes/` |
| **stavka.tv + Stake.com** | a retail book (~5.5% two-way vig) | server-rendered HTML match pages; Stake.com quote via freetips.com |

Pinnacle publishes **exactly our leg**: `spread ±1.5` (period 0) on a `bestOfX: 3` matchup
= map handicap −1.5 = P(2-0), plus `total 2.5` maps. Semantics cross-checked two ways: the
retail book's 4-way correct-score "0:2" price is *bit-identical* to its "−1.5" price
(1.73 and 1.73; 1.61 and 1.61 on a second match), confirming these are **series** map
handicaps, not map-1 round handicaps.

## Gate 0 — artifact hunt. **The artifact is found: phantom midpoints on dead books.**

Independent code path (`src/harvest.py`, `src/gate0.py`, `src/backtest.py`), nothing
reused from the idea's scan. Harvest: **17,338** resolved esports events (the idea got
16,959), **12,581** moneyline+handicap+totals triples, **10,980** true BO3, **10,471**
with full CLOB history. `prices-history` returned non-empty for **36,167 / 36,167** tokens
(0 failures) — the idea's 100% claim reproduces exactly.

### 0a. Leg semantics — the idea got these RIGHT

Typed only from `sportsMarketType`, then each `description` parsed with a regex and
checked against the token order. `outcomes[0]` of a `map_handicap` is the −1.5 team in
**12,581 / 12,581** cases; `outcomes[0]` of a `totals` is "Over" in **12,581 / 12,581**.
The handicap teams differ from the moneyline teams in **6 / 12,581** — all mid-season
team renames (`OKSavingsBank BRION` → `HANJIN BRION`, `DN Freecs` → `DN SOOPers`,
`Universe` → `STATE`, `Senshi Esports` → `Senshi Esports Club`), not label inversions.

### 0b. **"BO3" must be defined by the LEGS, not the title** — the largest structural artifact

A BO5's handicap is *also* "wins **2** or more maps", but its totals leg is "play **4** or
more". So the format is pinned down only by the **pair** (handicap margin 2 ∧ totals
threshold 3). Classifying on the title or on the handicap alone silently imports
**1,597 BO5/BO7 series** whose `HC_cover ⇔ win ∧ Under` identity is *mechanically*
different, and the identity rate collapses from 99.9% to 97.2%.

Restricted correctly to true BO3, the three-leg identity holds
**10,465 / 10,472 = 99.933%** — reproducing the idea's 99.93% on a 56%-larger sample.
The 7 violations include all 5 the idea named (`val-nbls-qe-2026-01-10`,
`cs2-uvs-lk-2026-01-09`, `cs2-ill-uvs-2026-01-12`, `cs2-fdb-bsta-2026-02-03`,
`cs2-vpp-lag2-2026-02-09`) plus 2 team-rename cases. **Gate 1 passes.**

### 0c. Timestamp alignment — **clean; this hypothesis is exonerated**

- Moneyline already collapsed (≤0.002 or ≥0.998) at T−1h: **9 / 9,592 = 0.09%**.
- Staleness of the T−1h read: median **582 s** (i.e. the 10-min bar grid — no gaps).
- Handicap pre-match path sd **0.0145** vs post-start sd **0.2076** — 14× larger after
  `gameStartTime`, so the field really does mark the start.
- Books open a median **20.2 h** before start.

The "T−1h" price is genuinely pre-match. There is no look-ahead. Good news for the
idea's method, and it is *why* the real artifact was so hard to see.

### 0d. Survivorship — a real but second-order omission

**508 / 10,980 BO3 triples (4.63%) settle 50-50**, not 1/0: the description's
"canceled / tie / delayed beyond 7 days" clause. All three legs go to 0.5 together, so
this is not asymmetric across legs — but a resolved-only ledger silently drops them, and
a 0.45 buy that hits one **loses 5c**. Among handicap books with >$5k volume the rate is
**3.30%** → a **0.165c/share** drag. Small, and it makes the trade worse, never better.

Not run as a separate LOO-isotonic fit, because gate 0 removed its input: on live books

## What survives

Nothing of the thesis. Three things worth keeping:

1. **An external sharp-line oracle, free and read-only.** Pinnacle's guest arcadia API and
   the Smarkets v3 API both serve full pre-match lines with no account. This is a
   general-purpose falsifier for *any* orakel sports/esports claim — run it before
   spending a slot, not after. → `wiki/` candidate, and the single most valuable output
   of this trial.
2. **"BO3 is a property of the legs, not the title."** Generalises: on Polymarket sports
   bundles, the *series format* must be inferred from the handicap margin and the totals
   threshold together.
3. **The phantom-midpoint magnitude, quantified.** 23% of these derivative legs never move
   pre-match; 85% are under $5k. Pooling them fabricated a +14pp edge in a family where
   the true edge is 0.0 ± 1.5pp. `thin-market-price-read.md` should carry this number.

## Recommendation

**Discard the variant** (`status = "retired"`), free slot 3. No applications are activated
and no prediction rows are submitted — the method's own gates say its signal is noise, and
submitting rows we believe are wrong would pollute the track record rather than test
anything the 34-pair Pinnacle comparison has not already settled far more sharply.

**Do not re-spend a slot on "trade Polymarket derivatives against a bookmaker line"
without first checking the fee arithmetic**: on the books that are actually tradeable the
median Polymarket−Pinnacle gap is **1.08pp**, against a **1.2c** taker fee plus a 1c
spread. The gap would have to be ~3× larger before the trade exists. Filed as a note, not
a proposal.
