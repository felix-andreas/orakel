## Gap 1 — between-family ρ: CLOSED, and the direction of the error is confirmed

07-30 said "the true effective n is below 173, so the failure at effective n is a lower bound
on the problem". **Confirmed, and now quantified — but not by the argument that was offered.**

| cluster level | clusters | k̄ | ρ | deff | `n_eff` | `q⁻` | vs `q*` = 0.8216 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| family = board × direction (07-30's unit) | 84 | 4.24 | 0.326 | 2.05 | **173** | 0.8095 | FAILS −1.21pp |
| board (asset × window) | 46 | 7.74 | 0.073 | 1.49 | 238 | 0.8191 | FAILS −0.25pp |
| **asset × direction** | **14** | **25.43** | **0.083** | **3.02** | **118** | **0.7950** | **FAILS −2.65pp** |
| asset | 7 | 50.86 | 0.000 | 1.00 | 356 | 0.8289 | clears +0.73pp |

**The answer: `n_eff` ∈ [118, 173], and the bound fails across that whole range** — by −1.2pp
at the family level and −2.7pp once families sharing an underlying *and* a direction are
pooled. WTI-DOWN-july and WTI-DOWN-week-of-jul-27 are one draw, and treating them as two was
worth 55 legs of imaginary evidence.

Two things in that table are worth more than the headline, and both are now in
`wiki/reference/clustering-coarser-is-not-safer.md`:

- **`ρ` at the board level (0.073) is a quarter of `ρ` at the family level (0.326)** — because
  a board mixes up-legs and down-legs, which are driven by *opposite* tails of the same path.
  Pooling them averages the correlation against its own opposite. **Direction, not the board,
  is the clustering unit.** 07-30 picked the right unit; this confirms it rather than
  refining it.
- **`ρ = 0.000` at the asset level is unidentifiability, not independence.** Seven clusters
  cannot estimate an ICC; the estimator truncates a badly-estimated negative variance
  component at zero. **The only level in the table that "clears" is the only one whose `ρ`
  cannot be measured.** It must not be quoted on Sunday, and it is named here so the gap is
  not mistaken for an omission later.

**Still open:** a genuine common-factor decomposition (one macro factor across assets) is not
done. But its sign is no longer in doubt and its magnitude is bracketed, so it is no longer a
blocker for 08-02 — it is a refinement.

## Gap 2 — fills folded into `q*`: CLOSED, and it is the decisive number

`q* = c = 1 − p` assumes we sell at the scored **midpoint**. We do not; a seller crossing to a
resting bid receives the **bid**. So the executable break-even is
`q*_exec = 1 − bid = q* + half-spread (+ fee)`.

Half-spread measured on the **65 live legs that passed the full book-quality gate** in the
3–50c band, across the 07-27…07-31 emission files (these are the only files that carry
`bid`/`ask`; `gate2_checkpoints.csv` carries `mid` only):

| asset | n | median half-spread | mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| wti | 16 | 1.00c | 1.02c |
| xauusd | 15 | 1.00c | 1.13c |
| xagusd | 12 | 1.00c | 0.93c |
| spy | 14 | 0.75c | 1.02c |
| nvda | 8 | 1.50c | 1.40c |
| **pooled** | **65** | **1.00c** | **1.07c** |

| scenario | `q*` | `q⁻` (nominal n=356) | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| mid fill, no fee (**07-30's number**) | 0.8216 | 0.8289 | clears +0.73pp |
| **sell at the bid, no fee** | **0.8316** | 0.8289 | **FAILS −0.27pp** |
| sell at the bid + 1c fee | 0.8416 | 0.8289 | FAILS −1.27pp |

**This is the cleanest statement of the whole promotion question, and it needs no correlation
argument at all: the nominal margin is +0.73pp and the median half-spread is 1.00pp. The edge
is smaller than the spread.** The break-even half-spread is **0.73c** — any book wider than
1.46c round-trip erases it, and the median gate-passing book is 2.0c wide.

So the two independent lines of attack agree and are not double-counting each other:

- ignore correlation entirely, price the real fill → fails by −0.27pp;
- ignore the fill entirely, price the correlation → fails by −1.21pp to −2.65pp;
- both together → fails by −2.21pp (family) to −3.65pp (asset × direction).

**Every cell in the joint table fails.** There is no combination of the two adjustments under
which the pooled sell-side bound clears.

*Fee caveat, stated rather than assumed:* the venue's current taker fee is not independently
verified here, so 0c/1c/2c are shown as a sensitivity. **The 0c row already fails**, so the
conclusion does not depend on the fee.

## Gap 3 — a capital model: CLOSED without a bankroll, because Kelly is scale-free

07-30 said this "needs a stated bankroll, which the firm has not set". It does not. The Kelly
fraction is a *fraction*, so the sizing question can be answered before the bankroll exists.

Buying the NO side at cost `c` for a payoff of 1: `f* = (q − c) / (1 − c)`.

| `q` used | value | `f*` |
|---|---:|---:|
| point estimate | 0.8680 | **+26.0%** of bankroll per independent bet |
| 95% lower bound, nominal n = 356 | 0.8289 | +4.07% |
| 95% lower bound, `n_eff` = 173 (family) | 0.8095 | **−6.77%** |
| 95% lower bound, `n_eff` = 118 (asset × dir) | 0.7950 | **−14.88%** |

**At any honest accounting of correlation the lower-bound Kelly stake is negative.** A negative
`f*` is not "size small"; it is "this bound says the other side of this trade is the better
one, or don't trade". That is a bankroll-free answer to "is the tail acceptable at our size",
and the answer is that there is no size at which it is acceptable *on this evidence*.

Two sizing rules that survive regardless:

1. **The unit is the family, not the leg.** k̄ = 4.24, so a per-leg cap of `x` is a per-draw
   cap of `4.24x`, and up to `12x` on the biggest family.
2. **The +26% point-estimate Kelly is the trap, not the opportunity.** It is what you get by
   plugging a favourite-side point estimate into a formula that assumes `q` is known. The
   entire distance between +26% and −6.8% is estimation error and correlation.

## What this does and does not settle

**Does:** all three gaps named on 07-30 are closed well enough to decide on. Gate 3
(fundability) fails at the executable price on nominal n alone. Gate 4 (tail at size) fails
across the full bracketed range of `n_eff`, and the capital answer is negative without needing
a bankroll.

**Does not:** this is still the gated 633-leg backtest sample, in one regime. It is the right
*shape* of answer, and Friday's rows are far too few to redo it. If Friday's out-of-sample
numbers disagree with this, **neither wins on its own** — that was true on 07-30 and today's
work does not change it.

**One thing that would change the verdict**, stated in advance so it is not invented later: a
band restriction to 35–50c, where 07-30 measured `q⁻` clearing by +5.2pp at +30.9% RoLC. That
band's edge is large enough to survive a 1c half-spread. But it is `n = 65` legs, it was found
by looking at a table of bands, and **it is a new pre-registration for a new cohort, not a
filter to be applied to a trial already in progress.**
