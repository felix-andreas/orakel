# Day-1 backtest — climate-nowcast/gistemp-era5 — 2026-07-24

**Recommendation: KILL.** Three of the idea's five kill conditions triggered, on 28
resolved instances (the falsification sketch asked for ≥20). The nowcast pipeline works
and is *better* than the idea promised (realized pre-print σ 0.038 vs the promised
0.056) — and it still loses to the market at every checkpoint, catastrophically in the
pre-print window the idea called "the richest sub-window". The crowd's confident modal
pricing is not overconfidence; it is earned: someone in this market nowcasts the GISTEMP
print to σ ≈ 0.014–0.018 °C three days before it posts, a precision an ERA5→GISTEMP
transfer cannot reach in principle (our irreducible transfer noise is ~0.036–0.040).
Every number reproducible from the frozen raw data
(`data/backtest-raw-2026-07-24.tar.gz.r2.json`) with `src/backtest.py` + `src/capacity.py`;
gate outputs in `results/{gates,capacity}.json`, per-checkpoint rows in
`results/checkpoint_records.json`, the trade tape of the sim in `results/trades.json`.

## Sample

28 resolved monthly bucket-family instances, Apr 2024 → Jun 2026, covering 26 unique
months (Apr 2025 and Oct 2025 each had two parallel lattices; Jul 2024 and — nominally —
Aug 2025 have no bucket family; the event `july-2025-temperature-increase-c-394` is
actually the **August 2025** family, mislabeled in its slug — every question in it reads
"in August 2025"; questions, not slugs, are authoritative). Per instance: full-series
CLOB prices-history for every leg (197 legs incl. live/ranking events, 0 empty), winners
from Gamma, tape for 8 instances, plus 50 unique Wayback vintages of `GLB.Ts+dSST.{txt,csv}`
(2024-03 → 2026-07) and the frozen ERA5/GISTEMP source snapshots from the market
researcher.

Point-in-time discipline: ERA5 dailies lagged 3 days; GISTEMP as-knowable-at-t = latest
Wayback vintage ≤ t (never today's file); all regressions fit on years strictly before
the predicted month; σ and bias per checkpoint fixed from a **pre-sample** 2015–2023
hindcast (108 months, before any backtested market existed), never tuned in-sample.
Checkpoints: day-15, day-21, month-end, pre-print (event `closedTime` − 72 h; verified
pre-print for all 28 — `closedTime` ≤ first Wayback capture of the print in every case).

## Gate 4 first (vintage integrity): backtest rebuilt on vintages — and it mattered

- Markets resolve exactly on the first print: **0/28** winner-vs-first-print mismatches.
- Today's revised file lands in a *non-winning* bucket for **9/28 instances** (Jun+Sep+Oct
  2024, Mar+Jun+Oct×2 2025, Feb+Mar 2026). A backtest scored on today's GISTEMP would
  mis-grade a third of the sample. Kill condition 4's "rebuild on vintages" branch was
  taken: ground truth here = actual market winners; targets = first prints.
- first_print − current: mean −0.005, sd 0.019 (n=28). GISTEMP revisions are half a
  bucket-width at 1σ — any future GISTEMP variant must treat the *first print* as the
  resolution variable, never the settled series.

ERA5-transfer can be. (Most plausibly they replicate GISTEMP from its own upstream
inputs — GHCN-M + ERSSTv5, both public with days of lag — which removes the
ERSST-vs-OSTIA / polar-interpolation transfer noise entirely.) Day-21 direction (0.53
priced vs 0.43 realized) leans thesis-ward but is 1.1σ from calibrated and monetized to
nothing (gate 3).

## Verdict

The idea's stated kill condition 1 — "kill if the market beats the model — then the
crowd already prices ERA5 properly" — is met at every checkpoint (and the crowd doesn't

well; 4 was absorbed into the build; 5 passes but sizes nothing worth having. Day 1 of a
10-day trial: recommending an early kill to free slot 2 rather than accumulating forward
predictions a backtest already grades as losers.
