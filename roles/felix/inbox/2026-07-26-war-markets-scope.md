## What happened

It is Polymarket's ~50 boards asking **"will the front line reach this exact spot by
&lt;date&gt;"** — one per Ukrainian town, several deadline legs each. Resolution is not a

## Why I am not promoting it tonight

Three reasons, and only the first one is mine to decide.

**1. It is a different kind of market than anything we have touched.** Oil barriers, film
review scores, temperature buckets and AI leaderboards are all impersonal. This is
territorial capture in an active war, and the thing we would be forecasting is how fast a
front line moves over towns people live in. Nothing in `CONSTITUTION.md` forbids it — we
take no positions and spend no money, so this is paper research on a public market — and I
do not think it is my call to rule the domain in or out on your behalf. You told me the CEO
is free within the constitution; I read a decision with this character as sitting just
outside that, in the same category as the constitution itself. **If you say go, I will run
it like any other idea.** If you would rather we did not, say so and I will mark the idea
`discarded-scope` with your reason recorded, so nobody re-proposes it in three weeks.

**2. The resolution source has a documented tamper history, and it points one way.** The
researcher pre-registered it as a kill gate rather than a footnote: on 15–16 November 2025
an unauthorised edit to the ISW map resolved a board worth over $1.3M Yes, vanished the next
morning, and was **never reversed by Polymarket**. A resolution feed that can be edited, and
a venue that will not correct it after the fact, is a hazard we have not faced before — our
other variants resolve on Pyth prices, a thermometer, or a review count. It biases against
the side this thesis takes.

**3. Gate 0 may kill it in an afternoon anyway.** The live ISW services expose only current
state. If we cannot reconstruct twelve months of weekly geometry, there is no physics
backtest and the idea collapses to a naive fade, which is not worth a slot. That test is
cheap and comes first whatever you decide.
