### The short reading

- **The two metrics disagree, exactly as DESIGN.md §3 predicts.** Highest cents/trade is `sniper` (10.47 on 405 trades, 7.43 days held); highest annualized return on locked capital is `harvest` (1654.0% on 463 trades, 3.12 days held). The capital-lockup rule, not the headline cents, is what separates them.
- **Sides.** On `mirror`, sells earned 7.75 c/trade (n = 1179, annROLC 547.8%) against 0.47 c/trade for buys (n = 888, annROLC -208.9%).
- **`patient` is not measured on the same sample as `fade`.** 684 signals had no observation 24h later and were dropped; 25% of those sit on markets that resolved in the token's favour — i.e. the dropped rows are enriched in the sell side's losses. Its 371.7% number is therefore an upper bound, and the honest comparison to `fade` does not exist in this data.
- **Capacity.** Peak deployment exceeded the stated bankroll for mirror (516%), gate (259%), kelly (540%), anchor (304%), fade (162%), patient (177%), sniper (386%), harvest (106%). Dollar PnL across policies is therefore not comparable; the rates are.
- **Every fill here is synthetic.** No row in this set carries a real book, so all of the above is priced at `mid ± assumed_spread/2`. Per DESIGN.md §4 these results are flagged, not celebrated: the ranking is a hypothesis about execution, not a measurement of it.

## Reading notes

- **Fills are never at mid.** Buys lift the ask, sells hit the bid; where only a midpoint exists the policy's `assumed_spread` is applied symmetrically and the trade is marked synthetic. A policy whose result rests on synthetic fills is flagged, not celebrated (DESIGN.md §4).
- **annROLC** = `Σ pnl / Σ (capital_locked × days_held) × 365`, the formula in DESIGN.md §3. It is a rate, not a fund return: multiply by `cap.eff` to see what the bankroll would actually have earned.
- **cap.eff > 100%** means the stated bankroll could not have funded the policy; the engine does not silently shrink positions to fit.
- Equity is bankroll plus *realized* PnL — open positions are not marked to market, because the signal sets carry no price for every day of every hold.
